📌 Project Overview

Customer retention is one of the most important growth drivers for SaaS businesses. Acquiring new customers is significantly more expensive than retaining existing ones, making churn analysis critical for long-term business success.

This project analyzes customer subscription behavior, churn events, support interactions, and feature adoption patterns to identify the key drivers of customer retention and attrition.

Using Power BI, an interactive dashboard was developed to provide actionable insights that can help improve customer satisfaction, reduce churn, and increase long-term recurring revenue.

🎯 Business Objectives

The project aims to answer the following business questions:

Why are customers leaving the platform?
Which customer segments are most likely to churn?
What factors influence customer retention?
How do support quality and customer satisfaction impact churn?
What actions can improve customer lifetime value and reduce customer attrition?

📈 Key Findings
Customer Retention Performance
Total Customers: 500
Active Customers: 390
Churned Customers: 110
Churn Rate: 22%
Retention Rate: 78%
Churn Drivers
Product feature limitations emerged as one of the leading churn drivers.
Support-related issues significantly contributed to customer attrition.
Customers who downgraded subscription plans exhibited higher churn tendencies.
Customer Satisfaction
Average Satisfaction Score: 3.98 / 5
Lower satisfaction scores were strongly associated with increased churn risk.
Product Engagement
Customers with higher feature usage generally demonstrated better retention.
Beta feature adoption remained relatively low, indicating opportunities for increased engagement.
💡 Business Recommendations
1. Improve Product Features

Address recurring feature-related concerns through continuous product enhancements and customer feedback analysis.

2. Strengthen Customer Support

Reduce ticket escalation rates and improve response times to increase customer satisfaction.

3. Monitor Downgrade Activity

Implement proactive retention campaigns targeting customers who downgrade subscription plans.

4. Increase Feature Adoption

Encourage feature utilization through onboarding programs, tutorials, and in-app guidance.

5. Build Early Churn Warning Systems

Leverage customer behavior, support interactions, and satisfaction metrics to identify at-risk customers before churn occurs.
